## Adding your organization

1. Fork the repository and add a row to the table above. Keep rows in alphabetical order by organization name.
2. Fill in all three columns:
   - **Organization**: your organization's name, linked to its website.
   - **Contact**: the GitHub handle of someone maintainers can reach with questions about your use.
   - **Description of Use**: one or two sentences on what you use Michelangelo AI for.
3. Open a pull request. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the contribution process, including the Contributor License Agreement.
